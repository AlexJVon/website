Portfolio Quest

Explore · Interactive Portfolio Navigator

Open in New Tab ↗

How to play:

Use W A S D or ↑ ↓ ← → to walk around, and E to interact with a terminal.

This isn't just a maze to wander — it's a working way to browse the site. Each terminal takes you to a different page: About, Projects, and Home will navigate you straight there, and Connect opens my social links.

How to play:

Type each terminal line exactly as shown before the 60-second clock runs out. Speed (WPM) and accuracy both feed your final score.

Firewall Defense

Tower Defense · Network Security

Open in New Tab ↗

How to play:

Click a firewall type, then click an open tile near the network path to build it. Click a placed tower to upgrade or sell it. Press Deploy Wave when you're ready — 15 waves total, with a boss wave every 5th. The network layout is randomized every run.

Neon Parkour

Procedural Runner · Grapple & Dash

Open in New Tab ↗

How to play:

Space or ↑ to jump, Shift to dash, E to grapple when an anchor lights up ahead. Some gaps are too wide to clear without the grapple. Pick a difficulty before you start — it scales gap sizes, hazards, and speed ramp-up, not your character's physics.

Labyrinth — Web Demo

First-Person Horror · 3D · Godot 4 · Floors 1–5

Open in New Tab ↗
// THE WAY DOWN A maze that rebuilds itself every floor — and things that hunt in the dark. Desktop browser · keyboard & mouse · ~110 MB download

How to play:

W A S D to move, mouse to look, Space to jump and climb, Shift to sprint, Ctrl to crouch, C to dodge-roll. E interacts, F uses the held item, G lights a flare, Tab opens your map, I your inventory. Click inside the game to capture the mouse; Esc pauses.

Descend through five procedurally generated floors. Sprinting is loud — some of what lives down here hunts by sound. Some of it only moves when you aren't looking.

Desktop browsers only (Chrome, Edge or Firefox). The full game has more floors, bosses and co-op.
